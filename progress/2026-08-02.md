## Milestone: M7 — Accessible interaction pass (Phase 6, Tasks 15–17)

### Planning

Created M7 and Phase 6 after an accessibility audit identified two concrete gaps: contribution-calendar cells use a button accessibility role despite having no action, and compact app controls need a consistent 44 × 44 pt touch-target contract. The milestone is deliberately planned, not started, because M6's physical-device validation remains incomplete.

Phase 6 is split into calendar semantics, shared touch-target implementation, and automated plus VoiceOver/Dynamic Type device validation. It explicitly keeps the calendar read-only rather than inventing a date-detail route outside the milestone's scope.
