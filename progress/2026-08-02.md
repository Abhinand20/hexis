## Milestone: M7 — Calendar day progress (Phase 6, Tasks 15–17)

### Planning

Ran a grilling session to turn the calendar concern into a concrete product flow: a past/current calendar day opens the existing History Day view for that date; future dates remain unavailable; the Day view keeps its per-practice summary and adds totals plus a chronological, read-only session list. Each new session captures its actual start time automatically. Details remains duration-only; there are no notes, backfills, time/date pickers, or post-save edits in this milestone.

Phase 6 is split into start-time persistence and migration, calendar-to-History navigation plus the bounded Day pager, and automated/device validation. The milestone is deliberately planned, not started, because M6's physical-device validation remains incomplete.

## History trend dashboard implementation

Redesigned the current-cycle History tab as a three-scale practice ledger:

- **Day** now pages across every elapsed cycle day and shows session/time totals plus a per-practice record.
- **Week** adds a seven-day rhythm chart, week-over-week session movement, target/time progress meters, and a strongest-day observation.
- **Cycle** adds active-day ratio, total effort, current and longest active-day runs, a recent weekly trend, target-normalized practice consistency, cadence-aware streaks, and the contribution map.

History now reloads when the tab regains focus, closing the stale-data gap after logging from Home. The new analytics remain pure domain calculations over cycle goals, revisions, and immutable logs; nothing is persisted as a duplicate aggregate.
